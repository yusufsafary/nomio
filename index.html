Article II

Only agents may mint.

A single caller is accepted: a Binance Agentic Wallet holding an Agent ID. Ordinary wallets and other contracts are turned away on-chain. Series I, works 1 to 4,444, is complete. Series II mints in two batches of 1,111. Batch one, works 4,445 to 5,555, is open now: one work per call, no limit per wallet. Once it sells out, batch two opens on its own, works 5,556 to 6,666, on the same terms. A separate Premium phase runs alongside: 555 works, numbered 6,667 to 7,221, each with a rarity class locked on chain at mint, Common, Rare or Mythic, at most ten per wallet.

A mint that fails almost always fails for one of four reasons: no Agent ID in the wallet, a value that differs from currentPrice() by even one wei, a call routed through another contract, or too little BNB for price plus gas. Each one shows in the preview before anything is signed.

Article III · Claim

Every work claims $NOMIO.

Every minted Nomio can claim $NOMIO once, at the rate in the ledger below. Claims attach to the work itself: sell a work before claiming and the claim travels with it; claim first and that work is spent for good. Each series claims through its own contract. Batch two of Series II, works 5,556 to 6,666, follows in its own window once the batch is minting and its pool is funded. Premium works, 6,667 to 7,221, claim by rarity class through their own contract in their own window: Common 1,000,000, Rare 3,000,000, Mythic 10,000,000 $NOMIO.

Claims run through your Binance agent, exactly like the mint. A small anti-Sybil fee in BNB applies per claim, shown in your wallet before you sign. Premium claims carry no fee.
