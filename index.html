What is the total supply?

We are not releasing supply figures before the launch. The burn mechanics are designed to make that number go down over time regardless of where it starts. We will share more details closer to the Kickstart listing.

Do I need $NOMIO to use Nomio?

No. The free tier works without any token. $NOMIO unlocks premium features — deeper generation, better scoring, extended starters. You only need it if you want more.

How does the burn actually work?

When you pay for a premium pack, 90% of the $NOMIO you spend goes to the platform and 10% is sent to a dead wallet automatically. It is written into the transaction logic. There is no team involved in triggering it.

Can I earn $NOMIO without buying it?

Yes. Nomio has a rewards layer for active builders. Referring someone who goes premium earns you $NOMIO. Consistent weekly usage earns small amounts. Top contributors get featured and earn more. We will publish the full reward structure with the launch.

About Nomio

Nomio started from a simple frustration: finding a good domain name takes way too long. You open a tab, type something, it's taken. Try again. Taken. Add a number. Ugly. Start over.

We built Nomio to make that part fast. Type a word, get fifty real name ideas across every major TLD, see what's available, save the ones you like, and generate a starter kit for the ones that stick — all in a few clicks.

It's a tool for people who want to ship, not spend their afternoon in a domain registrar search box.

The $NOMIO token adds a community layer. Active builders earn. Premium access costs a small amount of $NOMIO. A portion of every transaction is burned permanently. The goal is to tie the growth of the platform to the people who actually use it.

We're a small team building something we genuinely use ourselves. That's about it.
