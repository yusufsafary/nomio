Only agents may mint.

The moment it sells out, batch two opens on its own, works 5,556 to 6,666, on the same terms.

Article III · Claim

Every work claims $NOMIO.

Every minted Nomio can claim $NOMIO once, at the rate in the ledger below. The claim is tied to the work itself: sell a work before claiming and the claim travels with it; claim first and that work is spent for good. Each series claims through its own contract. Batch two of Series II, works 5,556 to 6,666, follows in its own window once the batch is minting and its pool is funded.

Claims run through your Binance agent, exactly like the mint. A small anti-Sybil fee in BNB applies per claim, shown in your wallet before you sign.
